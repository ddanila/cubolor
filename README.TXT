CUBOLOR FOR MS-DOS
==================

Roll the six-colored cube over the 3x3 floor. Every square takes the
color of the cube face touching it. Match the target picture at top right.

REQUIREMENTS
  386 or newer PC, EGA graphics, MS-DOS compatible operating system.

CONTROLS
  Numeric keypad 4 8 6 2  Roll in the screen direction
  Numeric keypad 7 or 1   Turn the view left
  Numeric keypad 9 or 3   Turn the view right
  U                       Undo
  R                       Restart puzzle
  Esc                     Quit to DOS

Movement remains relative to the screen after turning the view. There are
six 3x3 puzzles. The game runs at 50 frames per second when hardware permits.
